# Design

Spec for the combined direction. The visual reference is the mock at `mocks/dist/yvml/` (home, workshops list and grid, workshop detail; light and dark). Hand-written from the mock, not generated by `/impeccable document`. The mock is English only and has not been built in `apps/web`.

## Direction

Inventory's condensed serif titles and detail layout, adobe's home (alternating full-bleed panels), table's ruled list. Each material has its own color. Allo carries everything functional.

## Type

- **Titles and wordmark:** Instrument Serif (Google Fonts, OFL), 400 and italic. Display up to 6rem, tight leading (about .95), balanced.
- **Everything else:** Allo (OFL, `allo-font` on npm, `AlloVariable.woff2`). Variable axes `wght`, `ROND`, `opsz`. Body uses `ROND` 30. Nav 50, filters 70, buttons, links and the status pill 100.
- Tabular numerals for dates and prices. No monospace.
- Both faces are self-hosted. Licenses are in `mocks/dist/yvml/fonts/`.

## Color

Surface: paper `#ece8e1`, ink `#1b1712`, muted `#554e44`. Dark: warm black `#17130f`, text `#f1ebe1`, muted `#b9b0a2`. A toggle sets light or dark and remembers it; the default follows the system.

Each material has a text color per theme. Every text pair is at least 4.5:1 against its background. The panel tint is the material color mixed into the page background (22% in light, 26% in dark); ink or text on a tint is at least 8.4:1.

## Rules

- **Sold out stays visible and full contrast.** No show/hide toggle. Status is a text pill: open is filled, sold out is outlined.
- **Home features open workshops only**, in date order.
- **Listing:** the ruled list is the default (workshop, material, instructor, date, price, status); a grid of photos is the alternate view. Material chips filter. Row hover tints with the row's material.
- **Panels:** half photo, half material tint. Title, then material, date, instructor and price, then a "Register" link. No label above a title.
- **Detail:** photo left, title and a ruled spec table right (instructor, date, price, status), description, then Register when open.
- Controls are at least 44px. Focus is a 3px ring. Selection, caret, scrollbar and accent colors follow the theme. One motion: the hero photo reveals on load, off for reduced motion.
- Dropped from the mocks: inventory numbers ("No. 05"), the plate numbers, and the pastel-per-panel palette from adobe.

## Build notes for `apps/web`

- Theme tokens (palette, both faces, the material colors for both modes) go in `src/theme/`. The current theme is the shared neutral placeholder.
- Home panels, the ruled spec table, the material chip, the status pill and the list/grid toggle are YVML-specific and live in `apps/web`, from Park/Ark components where one fits. Nothing here goes in `@hmidmismi/ui`.
- Every string (Register, Sold out, Open, column heads, the theme toggle, view names) needs English and Spanish keys.
- The material colors key off the material slug already used in the workshop path facets (`/workshops/material/<slug>`).
- Not in the mock: events, people, support, about, the Spanish pages, empty states, loading states, the mobile menu (the nav wraps on small screens).
